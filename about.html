How we got here

Established in 1996.

Profiles Salon opened its doors in 1996 in a charming Victorian home in Conyers, with a simple idea: treat every client like family, and never stop getting better at the craft. Three decades later, that hasn't changed.

We specialize in cut and color, with stylists trained across every hair type, texture, and technique — plus beard trims and men's styles. We've been recognized locally as one of the area's top salons, and our stylists have earned their own recognition too. But what we're proudest of are the relationships: clients who started coming as kids and are still in our chairs decades later.
